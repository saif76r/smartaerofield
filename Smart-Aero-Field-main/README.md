# Smart Aero Field 🌾🛰️
**NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026**

> **Turning NASA Earth Observation into Agricultural Action**  
> A mobile-first agricultural intelligence platform and climate-adaptive planting-window advisory for smallholder farmers in Bangladesh.

---

## 🌟 Key Features
- **Planting-Window & Climate Advisory (Killer Demo):** Analyzes 25 years of NASA Earth observation data (2001–2026) to track shifts in usable rainfall onset (50mm soil recharge threshold) and cumulative Growing Degree Days (GDD Base 10°C). Deprecates outdated traditional schedules and provides climate-adjusted transplanting dates with statistical dual-distribution proof, ending on an actionable decision sentence in Bangla.
- **NASA Climatology ML Pipeline:** Custom machine learning inference microservice deployed on Render querying NASA POWER (MERRA-2 / GEOS-FP) and GPM IMERG telemetry for all 64 districts of Bangladesh.
- **Dual AI Leaf Disease Scanner:** Computer vision classification cross-validated with Google Gemini 2.5 Flash for foliar pathology diagnosis and organic/chemical prescriptions.
- **24/7 Gemini Agronomist:** Native Bangla conversational agricultural advisor providing personalized split-fertilizer schedules (Urea, TSP, MoP, Zinc) and water management guidance.

---

## 🛰️ NASA Earth Science Data Used
- **NASA POWER Agroclimatology (MERRA-2 / GEOS-FP):** 2m air temperature, precipitation, soil wetness, solar radiation, and GDD.
- **NASA GPM IMERG:** High-resolution precipitation constellation data for onset distribution validation.
- **NASA MODIS Terra/Aqua:** Land surface temperature and NDVI vegetation health indices.
